# Farm Data Research Agreement

## About This Repository

This repository contains a template for a **Farm Data Research Agreement** designed for farmers who provide farm data to researchers for agricultural research.

The agreement is intended to clearly explain how farm data will be collected, stored, protected, used, and shared while respecting the farmer's privacy and ownership of their data.

## What's Included

### `data_agreement.md`

The main file in this repository is a customizable **Farm Data Research Agreement** template.

It includes sections covering:

* **Purpose of the agreement** — Explains why farm data is being collected and how it will be used.
* **Data being collected** — Identifies potential types of farm data, including herd, milk production, health, breeding, nutrition, and management data.
* **Data ownership** — Clarifies that farmers retain ownership of the data they provide unless otherwise agreed.
* **Privacy and confidentiality** — Describes how identifying information about farms and farmers can be protected.
* **Data security** — Outlines practices such as secure storage, access controls, encryption, and backups.
* **Data sharing** — Explains when de-identified or aggregated data may be shared with other researchers or organizations.
* **Publication and research results** — Describes how research findings may be published while protecting farmer identities.
* **Withdrawal** — Explains how farmers can stop providing new data and what may happen to data that has already been used in research.
* **Data retention** — Describes how long research data may be kept and when it may be deleted.
* **Data access requests** — Gives farmers a way to ask how their data is being used and who has access to it.
* **Farmer and researcher consent** — Provides signature sections for both the farmer and researcher.

## FAIR Principles

The agreement incorporates the **FAIR data principles**, which promote responsible and useful data management.

### Findable

Data and its associated metadata should be organized and documented so that appropriate users can discover it.

The agreement addresses this through:

* Dataset descriptions
* Metadata
* Persistent identifiers
* Research repositories

### Accessible

Data should be accessible to appropriate users while protecting confidential and identifying information.

The agreement addresses this through:

* Controlled access
* De-identified datasets
* Access restrictions for sensitive information
* Publicly available metadata when appropriate

### Interoperable

Data should be organized in ways that allow it to work with other datasets and research systems.

The agreement encourages:

* Common file formats
* Standard units of measurement
* Clearly defined variables
* Agricultural terminology and standards
* Documentation of data transformations

### Reusable

Data should contain enough information and documentation to allow appropriate future research and reuse.

The agreement addresses this through:

* Data dictionaries
* Documentation of collection methods
* Data quality information
* Dataset versioning
* Licensing and terms of use
* Proper citation of datasets and research

## How to Use This Template

This agreement is intended to be **customized for individual research projects**.

Before using it, researchers should replace the placeholder information, such as:

```text
[Project Name]
[Researcher Name]
[Organization]
[Email Address]
[Date]
```

Researchers should also modify the sections describing the specific types of data being collected, how the data will be shared, and how long the data will be retained.

## Why a Data Agreement Is Important

Farm data can contain valuable information about animals, production, management practices, finances, and farm operations. Farmers should understand what happens to their information after they provide it to researchers.

A data agreement helps establish clear expectations between farmers and researchers by explaining:

**What data is collected → How it is protected → How it is used → Who can access it → How it may be reused**

This can help build trust between farmers and researchers while supporting responsible data management.

## Important Note

This document is a **research agreement template**, not legal advice. Research institutions should review and adapt the agreement to comply with applicable laws, institutional policies, research ethics requirements, and data protection requirements before using it with participants.
